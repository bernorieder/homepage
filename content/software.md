Over the years I have written quite a number of research tools, mainly for the data-driven analysis of social media platforms. My main goal is to gain a deeper understanding of the logics embedded in these platforms and their APIs, and writing research tools is an excellent way to pursue this kind of exploration. Nothing beats first-hand experience.

Development and maintenance of some of these tools are funded by the Dutch [Platform Digitale Infrastructuur Social Science and Humanities](https://pdi-ssh.nl/) as part of the [CAT4SMR](https://cat4smr.humanities.uva.nl/) project.

<div class="note">

**Support.** Most tools have basic descriptions or FAQ sections, and there are instruction videos on my [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@BernhardRiederAmsterdam). Questions, feature requests, and high-quality bug reports are much appreciated, but only if filed via [GitHub](https://github.com/bernorieder/). Please do not use email or social media. If you have never written a bug report, please read [this piece](https://www.chiark.greenend.org.uk/~sgtatham/bugs.html), before submitting.
